### 1.3 A counting caveat this round made visible

All three tools here take CC from radon, and radon attributes a nested `def`'s
branches **to the nested function**. A function whose body is mostly one closure
therefore reads low: `services/undo_service.py::_apply_db_command` is CC 5 in the
audit while the same body measured by `gate_check`'s cognitive pass is **31**.
`current_audit.py` reports the nested `work` separately (CC 14), so nothing is
hidden — but "CC ≤ 10" alone is not sufficient evidence that a function is
readable, and this report uses `cognitive` and `LOC` as the tie-breaker for the
remaining tail (which is why `undo_service` is listed above even though its CC is
under the gate).
